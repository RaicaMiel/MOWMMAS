Track Submission

Track your submission

Enter your reference number and the mobile number you used on the form. You will see the latest status and any messages from the health workers.

Look up a submission

It starts with MOW-, for example MOW-R-2026-00012.

The number you entered on the form.

Where is my reference number?

  • On the confirmation page you saw after sending your form.
  • In the SMS messages about your submission.
  • Lost it? Call the facility you sent your form to. Give them your name and mobile number.

What each status means

Your status changes as the facility works on your form. You get an SMS at each important step.

Donation offer

  1. Submitted

    We sent your offer to the facility.

  2. Under review

    A health worker is checking your details.

  3. Screening scheduled

    Go to your health check on the date in your SMS.

  4. Donation accepted

    You can donate. The facility will tell you how.

  5. Completed

    All done. Thank you for donating!

OrDeclinedThe facility can’t accept your milk right now. Call them to ask why.

Donor milk request

  1. Submitted

    We sent your request to the facility.

  2. Under review

    A health worker is checking your request.

  3. Approved

    Your baby will get donor milk.

  4. Ready for pick-up

    Go to the facility to collect the milk.

  5. Completed

    All done. Your request is finished.

OrDeclinedThe facility can’t give milk right now. Call them to ask why.

Question

  1. Submitted

    We sent your question to the facility.

  2. Answered

    A health worker replied by SMS or a call.

  3. Closed

    All done. You can ask a new question anytime.

Need an answer today? Call the facility. Its number is on the facility page in Hospitals Near Me.
