About MOWMMAS

A bridge between mothers and health facilities

MOWMMAS brings mothers and authorized health personnel onto one web application, so information about breast milk services is easier to find and keep up to date.

For mothers

Find · Check · Contact

  • Browse participating hospitals and health centers near you
  • Check facility details before calling or visiting
  • Donate, request, or inquire through simple forms
  • Receive SMS updates about your submission

For health workers

Manage · Update · Notify

  • Keep facility details, hours, and HMB status accurate
  • Review donation and request information
  • Update service and availability status
  • Send SMS notifications when needed

Important: MOWMMAS does not function as a Human Milk Bank.

It only provides information, communication, referral support, and notifications. The actual collection, processing, storage, and distribution of breast milk are handled by authorized health facilities and Human Milk Banks.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you get started.

Does MOWMMAS give out or store breast milk?

No. MOWMMAS is an information and referral service. Breast milk is collected, processed, stored, and distributed only by authorized health facilities and Human Milk Banks.

Do I need to install an app?

No. MOWMMAS is a web application, so you can open it in any web browser. SMS notifications arrive on any mobile phone, even without mobile data.

Who reviews the information I submit?

Authorized health workers or administrators of the participating health facility. They manage your donation, request, or inquiry and update its status.

How will I know about updates?

When a health worker makes an important update, MOWMMAS sends an SMS to the mobile number you provided. You can also check your status or contact the facility directly.

Is MOWMMAS free to use?

Yes. Using MOWMMAS is free. Any fees for screening or processing are set by the health facility, so please ask them directly.
