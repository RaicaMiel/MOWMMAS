Step 1 of 6 · Breast Milk Information

Breast milk support, made easier to find

MOWMMAS helps mothers in Antique find health facilities, and a MOWMMAS administrator reviews your form.

MOWMMAS helps you

  • Find health facilities and their services
  • Send a donation inquiry, a receiving inquiry or a question
  • Get referral support from a MOWMMAS administrator
  • Receive updates by SMS

MOWMMAS does not

  • Collect, store or give out breast milk
  • Decide who can donate or receive milk
  • Promise that donor milk is available
  • Replace advice from your doctor or health worker

The next steps show what happens when you donate, receive or ask.

Step 2 of 6 · Learn About Donation/Receiving

Donating and receiving, step by step

Both start with a short form. A MOWMMAS administrator reviews it and tells you what to do next.

Donating

For mothers with extra breast milk

  1. 1 Send a donation inquiry
  2. 2 A MOWMMAS administrator gives the next steps or a referral
  3. 3 Contact the referred facility to confirm requirements and schedule
Find a facility to donate

Receiving

For babies who need donor milk

  1. 1 Send a receiving inquiry
  2. 2 A MOWMMAS administrator reviews it and gives referral information
  3. 3 Contact the referred facility to confirm availability and requirements
Start a receiving inquiry

Screening, collection and giving out of donor milk are done only by authorized facilities and milk banks.

Step 3 of 6 · Inquire About Services

Not sure yet? Ask first

Send a question to a MOWMMAS administrator. It’s free, and they reply by SMS or a call.

You can ask about

  • Donor milk availability
  • Requirements and documents
  • How to donate milk
  • How to request milk
  • Breastfeeding help
  1. Choose a facility the one your question is about
  2. Write your question in your own words
  3. Get your answer by SMS or a call
Ask a question

Step 4 of 6 · Select/Find Appropriate Facility

Find the facility that fits your need

Health Facilities Near Me lists the facilities in Antique with their location, contact number, hours and services.

What the labels mean

  • Yes — documentedThe service is documented for this facility.
  • Not VerifiedNot confirmed yet. Call the facility to ask.
  • Not offeredThe facility doesn't offer this service.
  • Verified HMBA Human Milk Bank that has been verified.
  • HMB: Not VerifiedListed as a Human Milk Bank, but not verified yet. Call to ask.
  • No Confirmed HMBNot confirmed as a Human Milk Bank. Call to ask.
  • N/AListed for information only. Contact the facility directly.
Browse health facilities

Tip: use your location to see the nearest first.

Step 5 of 6 · Referral/Communication Support

A MOWMMAS administrator guides your next step

A MOWMMAS administrator reviews your form. If another facility fits your need better, you are referred there.

What a receiving inquiry’s status shows

  1. New Receiving InquiryYour form is sent
  2. Under ReviewA MOWMMAS administrator checks it
  3. Referral NeededFinding the right facility
  4. Information SentThe details come to you
  5. CompletedAll done

A donation inquiry starts as New Donation Inquiry and shows Referral/Next Steps Provided at the third step.

Got a referral? Contact that facility to confirm current availability, requirements, and schedule.

Step 6 of 6 · SMS Notification

Updates come to your phone

MOWMMAS texts the mobile number on your form at each important step. No app needed.

MOWMMAS

MOWMMAS: We received your receiving inquiry. Ref: MOW-R-2026-00012. MOWMMAS will review it and text you the next steps.

MOWMMAS Update: Your receiving inquiry MOW-R-2026-00012 has been reviewed. Please contact [facility name] at [number] to confirm current availability, requirements, and schedule.

You get a text when

  • Your form is received, with your reference number
  • A MOWMMAS administrator updates your status
  • You are referred to a facility
