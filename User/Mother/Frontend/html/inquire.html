Ask a health facility a question

Not sure where to start? Ask about donor milk, what to bring, or how donating and requesting work. Your question goes straight to the facility’s health workers, and they reply by SMS or a call.

How to inquire

Four short steps. You can always call the facility instead.

  1. 1

    Choose a facility

    Pick the hospital or health center you want to ask, above or from Hospitals Near Me.

  2. 2

    Write your question

    Choose a topic and ask in your own words: English, Filipino or Kinaray‑a.

  3. 3

    Add your number and send

    You get a reference number right away, like MOW-I-2026-00001.

  4. 4

    Get your answer

    A health worker replies by SMS or a call. Check anytime on Track Submission.
